# Banned AI Giveaways

Avoid these patterns in public posts:
- "In today's rapidly evolving landscape"
- "Delve"
- "Unlock the power"
- "Transformative potential"
- "Seamless integration"
- "Cutting-edge solution"
- "Harness the power"
- "Game changer"
- "Revolutionize"
- "Navigate the complexities"
- "Ever-evolving"
- "Robust ecosystem"
- Overly symmetrical three-part endings.
- Too many em dashes or semicolons.
- Vague grand claims without a concrete source.
